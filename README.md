# Privacy Policy for GeoBrain

Last updated: October 2026

Zbyt Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the GeoBrain mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

GeoBrain does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App functions entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to operate.

**No personal data is collected.**

**No usage analytics are tracked.**

**No advertising identifiers are used.**

**No data is shared with third parties.**

All app data (quiz progress, scores, streaks, badges) is stored locally on your device only.

## Children's Privacy

GeoBrain is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.
